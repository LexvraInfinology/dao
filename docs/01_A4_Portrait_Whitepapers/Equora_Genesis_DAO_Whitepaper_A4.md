## 3. What is a DAO? (Decentralized Autonomous Organization)

A **DAO** is an organization run entirely by transparent, self-executing code on the blockchain instead of a centralized company board:
- **No Boss, No Admin:** The rules cannot be changed by any person, developer, or company.
- **Direct Wallet Settlement:** Money is never held in a company bank account. Payouts push directly into your personal Web3 wallet.
- **True Co-Ownership:** All 100 Genesis DAO seats hold an equal vote (1 vote = 1.0%) over protocol parameters.
- **Soulbound Pass:** Your DAO seat is an immutable, non-transferable ERC-721 token bound to your wallet, preventing whales or corporations from buying up council control.
