--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE 3X ($900) CAP & 48-HOUR RE-TOPUP WINDOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Total cumulative payout capped at $900 per $300 seat.
  - Once $900 is reached, an immutable 48-hour countdown timer begins.
  - Depositing a $300 re-topup resets accumulated earnings to $0 and refreshes the
    3X cap for another $900 cycle.
  - Re-topup fee of $300 is pushed into the 35% DAO Treasury Pool.
  - If unpaid within 48 hours, the seat is marked vacant and opened for reassignment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. AUTOMATED 1-100 VACANCY SCAN ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Contract iterates from Seat 1 through 100 upon every new joinDAO() call.
  - Incoming members occupy the lowest vacant seat index first.
  - Vacated high-priority positions (e.g. Seat #3, Seat #15) are dynamically filled
    by fresh capital, preventing structural gridlock.
  - The DAO queue is permanent; the former 15-day inactivity timeout is eliminated.
